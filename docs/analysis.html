Partisan Lean Index

Measures the baseline partisan environment of each district. It is computed as a weighted blend of the reallocated 2024 presidential margin and the re-tabulated 2020 presidential return, normalized against national popular vote baselines:

Index = (0.70 × 2024 Pres. Margin Normalized) + (0.30 × 2020 Pres. Margin Normalized)

Edge-Case Handling & Jungle Primaries

Standard two-party general elections do not occur in all seats. To prevent mathematical distortions, we apply a strict 3-tier hierarchy:

  • Standard Contest: Signed two-party margin: (Dem - Rep) / (Dem + Rep).
  • Top-Two / Jungle Primaries (CA & WA): In general contests between members of the same party (D vs D or R vs R), we aggregate all Democratic vs. Republican votes cast during the all-party primary stage as the benchmark.
  • Unopposed / Uncontested: When a major party fielded no candidate, the 2024 Presidential Margin is imputed as the true partisan floor. Non-standard races are flagged with an asterisk (*).

Impact on Targeted Districts

Definitions for the four topline metrics in the default sidebar overview:

  • Mean Partisan Shift: The absolute average shift (|Margin Shift|) across all targeted boundaries, measuring the raw magnitude of the cartographic changes.
  • Median Partisan Lean: The median partisan lean of all targeted seats, indicating the center of gravity of the redrawn maps.
  • Mean Partisan Shift (Net): The directional signed mean (Σ Margin Shift / N), revealing which party captured net territory overall.
  • Median New District: The exact median seat when sorting all altered districts by post-redistricting presidential margin.

Tipping Point & Seat Advantage

Calculates chamber-control thresholds and spatial seat bias across the 435 U.S. House seats:

  • Tipping Point District: The 218th seat when ranking all 435 congressional districts from most Democratic to most Republican. If a party wins the tipping point seat, they secure the House majority.
  • Generic Ballot Advantage (Seat Bias): Measures systemic cartographic tilt by subtracting the national popular vote share from the majority party's projected seat share:
    Advantage = (Seats Won / 435) - National 2-Party Vote Share
